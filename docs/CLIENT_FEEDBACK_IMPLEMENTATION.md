# Client feedback implementation

This checklist maps the client’s 18-point review to the application. It also identifies launch dependencies that must not be fabricated.

1. **Personal, relationship-led design** — The homepage now leads with Ruhan’s portrait, name, role, languages and personal guidance. The domain is treated as a personal marketing website, not a separate brokerage.
2. **Hero conversion** — The hero combines approved Miami editorial imagery, Ruhan’s official BHS profile portrait, `Search Properties` as the primary action, and `Talk to Ruhan` as the personal action.
3. **Lighter luxury palette** — The design tokens and new homepage sections use deep navy, warm ivory, sand and restrained gold with editorial typography and minimal glass effects.
4. **Homepage sequence** — Hero → four primary journeys → property search → Market Today → Meet Ruhan → areas → BHS credibility → relocation → authentic testimonials → guides → final CTA.
5. **Prominent property search** — Location, sale/rent, maximum price and bedroom filters route into the IDX-ready search page and preserve selected filters.
6. **Market Today near the top** — The market snapshot is now directly below search. It continues to fail closed and never invents missing figures.
7. **Short emotional About** — The homepage story summarizes Ruhan’s Mumbai background, business/technology, hospitality, Miami MBA and relationship-focused approach, with the full story linked.
8. **Brown Harris Stevens credibility** — A dedicated dark section includes the verified founding year (1873), privately held positioning, support resources and official BHS profile link. All BHS wording/assets remain subject to broker approval.
9. **NY/NJ → Miami funnel** — A major homepage relocation section collects origin, buy/rent status, budget, timeframe and contact basics, then pre-fills the complete consent-based relocation form.
10. **Qualified lead forms** — Existing adaptive three-step forms retain intent, budget, location, property type, timing, financing, contact preference, consent and source attribution.
11. **WhatsApp visibility** — Desktop floating WhatsApp, mobile sticky WhatsApp, inquiry delivery choices, property-page WhatsApp and assistant handoff are present.
12. **AI lead qualifier** — The assistant starts with six intent choices, gathers relevant criteria one at a time, uses only approved CMS/IDX context and offers WhatsApp, call or text after useful qualification.
13. **Property-page conversion** — The sticky property panel now shows Ruhan’s identity and portrait with Ask/Schedule, WhatsApp, Call and Email actions. Submitted leads carry property metadata automatically.
14. **Visual area cards** — Six priority areas appear as large editorial image cards with objective housing/access tags and links to full area guides.
15. **Testimonials** — Only published CMS testimonials render. With no approved testimonials, the site shows a trust statement instead of fabricated reviews.
16. **Simplified navigation** — Public navigation is now Buy, Sell, Rent, Areas, Market, About and Contact, plus a persistent `Talk to Ruhan` action.
17. **Mobile conversion priority** — Mobile uses three sticky actions: Call, WhatsApp and Search. The assistant remains available as a compact floating button above that bar.
18. **Lead funnel and measurement** — Existing first-touch UTM persistence, lead metadata, page/search/form/contact events and CRM storage remain connected to every conversion flow.

## Production dependencies

- Connect an approved Miami REALTORS/MLS/IDX provider before showing live listings or listing imagery.
- Publish market figures only after a source, geography, reporting period and updated date are configured.
